# Video gallery

Every video puts the **real clip next to the model**, on the same clock (time zero = the moment the ball separates from the tin; slowed 8x). Clips 0536 and 0537 were used to fit the models; **0538 and 0539 are held-out** (never used for fitting). If you only watch two, watch the first two rows of the continuum section.
